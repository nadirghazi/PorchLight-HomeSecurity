About Porchlight

We're the front door, not the installer.

Porchlight is a free connection service. We don't install cameras or alarms ourselves — we help homeowners find the independent local pros who do.

What Porchlight actually is

Finding a home security installer usually means scrolling through directories, filling out forms, and waiting for callbacks that may or may not come. Porchlight shortcuts that: call one number, describe what you want done, and we point you to an independent local installer who covers your area and your type of job.

We are a referral service, not a security company. We don't employ the installers, set their prices, or perform any installation work ourselves. Each installer in our network runs their own independent business.

How we make it faster

Instead of a form that sits in an inbox, a phone call gets you a real answer in one conversation. We ask a few quick questions — your ZIP code, what you're looking to install, and any details that matter — then connect you toward installers who fit.

What we ask you to double-check

Because every installer is independent, it's on you as the homeowner to confirm they carry the license and insurance required for your job before work begins. We encourage every homeowner to ask directly and get it in writing if that matters to you.

Where we operate

Porchlight works with installers across all 50 states — see our Areas We Serve page for a regional breakdown, or just call and we'll tell you what's available where you live.
