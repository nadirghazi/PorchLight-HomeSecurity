Security Cameras

Wired or wireless bullet and dome cameras, placed to cover front doors, driveways, side gates and backyards. Installers can wire cameras to an existing system or set up a standalone wireless setup with app access.

  • Day & night recording options
  • Wired or battery/solar wireless
  • Mobile app viewing setup

Floodlight Cameras

A camera and motion-activated flood light in one fixture. The light kicks on the moment someone walks into range, so you get both a warning and a recording.

  • Motion-triggered lighting
  • Wide-angle coverage for driveways & yards
  • Hardwired to existing fixtures where possible
